Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

openclaw digest is a personal tool. Only its owner signs in to it, and it only touches the owner's own Google accounts.

What it accesses

  • Gmail, read only: sender, subject and a short preview of new messages, to summarise new mail for the owner, and a whole message when the owner asks for it. It never sends, moves, labels or deletes email.
  • Google Calendar: it creates or moves an event only after the owner approves that change.

Where the data goes

The data is processed on the owner's own private server and by the AI model the owner uses to write the summaries. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Keeping and removing access

Summaries stay on the owner's server. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
