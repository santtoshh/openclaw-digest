openclaw digest

openclaw digest is a personal assistant tool. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission it:

  • reads the owner's own Gmail, read only, to write a short summary of new mail (sender, subject and a short preview), and reads a whole message when the owner asks;
  • adds or moves events on the owner's own Google Calendar, only after the owner approves each change.

It never sends, moves or deletes email.
